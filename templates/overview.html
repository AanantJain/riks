The story

One engine. Two exams. One loop a night.

Indian families already buy coaching. What they still do not have is a governor for the remaining days: what to sit tonight, why it matters for the mark sheet, and what the parent is allowed to help with.

The problem

Coaching and YouTube are infinite. Time is not. Parents add sheets. Students pick at random. Progress looks busy and the exam date does not move.

What RIKS is

A family product that holds the clock, the marking scheme, and a personal skill model. It outputs a minimum set before sleep and an in-app paper. The plan rewrites from that paper. Empty bars on day one are the point: nothing is faked.

1

See today

Open Today. Read the named set. That is the sitting.

2

Sit the paper

Open Practice (contest) or Daily exam (INI-CET). Submit. Get topic-level notes.

3

Coach the leak

Open Coach. Help the named chapter. Do not pile random work. Do not scold a single %.

Two desks, same loop

Aanant is contest rating (Codeforces / CSES). Board shows projected rating versus the goal. Live rounds stay on the actual judge; RIKS grades the home paper.

Nikita is INI-CET. Desk shows an 8-week subject block, hospital-duty hours, and progress versus a rank band. Tick Start from scratch only if the date or the story of past papers must be wiped.

Desks (top left) always returns to pick a desk. Leave on Nikita's desk returns to the chooser; it does not clear the family password.

What to show an investor in five minutes

  1. Chooser: the thesis, then open a desk.
  2. Desk: days left, progress at 0% if no papers yet.
  3. Today → sit the paper → result page names topics, not character.
  4. Coach: “You can / Please do not.”
  5. Other desk: same loop, different exam.
