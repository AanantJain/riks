Overview

How to use RIKS

One family app. Open the link, type the password once, then pick a desk. Switch at the top always comes back to the chooser.

Choose a desk Aanant Nikita

0. First open

  1. Open the RIKS URL (or add it to the Home Screen).
  2. If asked, enter the family password.
  3. On Who is opening RIKS? pick Aanant, Nikita, or Nikita's coach view.

1. Aanant - typical day

Finish today's named problems and the in-app daily exam.

  1. Board - clock, projected rating, strong / needs work.
  2. Use Tell RIKS the target only when the contest date, rating goal, or strong/weak topics changed. Then rebuild.
  3. Today - the bot lists the minimum set before sleep.
  4. Practice or the daily exam - try questions in RIKS; it grades them and updates the plan.
  5. For live rounds: Periodic (Codeforces) and Library.
  6. If yesterday was unfinished, the bot asks why - answer that; it is not a scolding screen.

Weekly: sit a real Codeforces/AtCoder round, then upsolve. Coach shows which module to help with.

2. Nikita - first time

Set the real INI-CET date. Start at zero - no fake papers.

  1. Open Nikita's desk.
  2. On Desk, scroll to Tell RIKS the plan: exam date, target band, hospital duty / test series, strong and weak subjects.
  3. Optionally tick Start from scratch.
  4. Tap Rebuild my schedule.
  5. Open 8-week plan once to see Week 1-8 (anatomy through the full 200-Q mock).

Progress vs target stays 0% until she sits a paper. That is correct.

3. Nikita - typical day

  1. Desk - days left, this week's focus, minimum set before sleep.
  2. Today - bot: today's chapters and why they matter.
  3. Daily exam - finish the MCQs. Explanations appear; tomorrow's list can change.
  4. If duty or a mock ran long: Log leave on Today.
  5. If yesterday was incomplete: say why (stuck, test series, duty, energy).

Weekly: on the Periodic day, take that week's mock (50 / 100 / Week 8: 200 Q). Change the exam date anytime in Tell RIKS the plan, then rebuild.

4. Parent / coach

  • Aanant - open Coach on his desk. Read standing, today's list, You can / Please do not. Help the named module; do not pile random sheets.
  • Nikita - open Coach on her desk, or Coach view from the chooser. Help the named subject (for example redo Pharma MCQs). Do not scold a single percentage.
  • If a day was missed, ask them to open Today and log it. RIKS already names the catch-up.

5. Switch people on one phone

  1. Tap Switch (or the RIKS logo).
  2. Pick the other desk.
  3. Leave on Nikita's desk only returns to the chooser. It does not clear the family password.

What good use looks like

  • One sitting a day: Today, then the daily exam, then sleep.
  • Change the plan only when the date, target, or load changed - not every evening.
  • Coach reads topic leaks, not character.
  • Progress moves only after real papers. Empty bars on day one are correct.
